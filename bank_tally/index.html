Add or change a mapping

A mapping (alias) tells the app which Tally ledger a name in the bank narration belongs to — e.g. ARYAN ISPAT AND POWER PRIVATE LI → Aryan Ispat & Power Private Ltd.. It applies to receipts and payments on every account, and is remembered on this Mac. Type the name the way the bank shows it; it's matched ignoring case, spacing and words like M/S, PVT, LTD.
